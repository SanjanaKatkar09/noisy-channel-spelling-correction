ERROR ANALYSIS
==============

The Noisy Channel Model correctly corrected 23 out of 24
test cases.

One error was observed:

Misspelled word:
algoritms

Expected correction:
algorithms

Noisy Channel prediction:
algorithm

Standard Spell Checker prediction:
algorithms

Reason:
Both "algorithm" and "algorithms" are valid candidates based
on edit distance. The Noisy Channel Model selected "algorithm"
because its estimated corpus probability was higher than the
probability of "algorithms".

This shows a limitation of using a small corpus for estimating
word probabilities. Context-aware language modeling or a larger
corpus could improve the correction of such cases.
